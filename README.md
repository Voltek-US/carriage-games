# Carriage Games

Fourteen games for 2–6 players, played phone to phone with no internet: Chess, Car Racing, Tank Battle, Air Hockey, Texas Hold'em, Liar's Dice, Yahtzee Duel, Checkers, Othello, Gomoku, Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, Mancala, Nine Men's Morris and Quoridor.

Phones connect directly over a shared hotspot or Wi-Fi (WebRTC data channels, paired by QR code; the host phone links all players and relays messages). Poker, Liar's Dice, Yahtzee, Car Racing and Tank Battle support up to 6 players. After the first visit the app is cached by a service worker and works fully offline.

Chess board: [Chessground](https://github.com/lichess-org/chessground) (GPL-3.0) with cburnett pieces (CC BY-SA 3.0, Colin M.L. Burnett). Fonts: Fraunces (SIL OFL). QR: jsQR, qrcode-generator (MIT).
